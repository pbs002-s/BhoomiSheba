Modernizing land administration is one of the most complex challenges in digital governance. I'm excited to share **BhoomiSheba** — a comprehensive Digital Land Management & Cadastral Automation Platform built for Bangladesh (ভূমি সেবা ও অটোমেশন পোর্টাল). 🇧🇩 

This isn't just a record-keeping app; it's a geospatial-first, event-driven solution designed to automate and cross-audit the entire land lifecycle—from e-Mutations (ই-নামজারি) to LD Tax Payments (e-Dakhila).

✨ **Core Capabilities:**
• **Geospatial Intelligence:** Built on PostGIS spatial geometry for accurate vector mapping and boundary encroachment detection.
• **Automated Cross-Audit:** Reconciles records across e-Parcha, DLRS cadastral maps, and Sub-Registry deed vaults instantly.
• **Event-Driven Workflows:** Powered by n8n and PostgreSQL CDC to automate citizen SMS notifications and officer assignments.
• **Beautiful UI:** A full-screen React dashboard featuring Anek Bangla typography and interactive vector canvases.

I built this as a proof-of-concept for how enterprise-grade GovTech can be fast, transparent, and scalable.
